Amy Heyrin Choi is an undergraduate student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at KAIST, and is spending the fall 2026 semester at UT Austin as an exchange student in Computational Engineering. Her research interests include robotics, optimization, and reinforcement learning. In the CLeAR Lab, she works on optimization under uncertainty and contributed to InneR, a regularization method for robot imitation learning.

Back at KAIST, Amy built optimization models for assigning public-health physicians across Korea and has worked on multi-agent reinforcement learning. In her free time, Amy enjoys jogging, going on picnics, and baking.
